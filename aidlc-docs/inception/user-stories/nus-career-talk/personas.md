# NUS Career Talk - Personas

These are task-based archetypes derived from approved requirements, not invented biographies or research participants.

## P1 - Presenter

Goal: deliver an engaging Vietnamese career guidance talk in 10–15 minutes, explain CS versus Computer Engineering, introduce NUS CS and all eleven focus areas, and invite further exploration.

Needs: projection-readable narrative, predictable previous/next and keyboard controls, optional fullscreen, three featured demonstrations, clear view switching and a practical closing challenge. Assumes access to a browser and loaded website assets, not a reliable live API.

Constraints: limited live time; the presenter cannot demonstrate all eleven areas deeply during the main talk. Fullscreen can be unavailable. The topic must remain understandable without university course-code knowledge.

Success: completes the core story in a rehearsal within 10–15 minutes, can operate controls without accidental slide changes inside demos, and leaves students with a next step.

Story mapping: US01, US02, US03, US04, US05, US07, US08, US09. US06 supports answering follow-up questions.

## P2 - High-school student

Goal: understand computing pathways and find a connection between personal interests, real applications and possible careers.

Needs: natural Vietnamese, explained terminology, familiar examples, official English field/job names, controllable visual demonstrations and an achievable beginner project. No prior coding background is assumed.

Contexts: watches a projected talk, then independently explores the page on a phone or computer. May navigate using a keyboard or prefer reduced motion; these are access needs within the persona rather than separate invented audiences.

Constraints: dense text and unexplained jargon reduce understanding. Job examples must describe possibilities without implying guaranteed outcomes. Live animations must have understandable text equivalents.

Success: describes a meaningful CS/CE difference and overlap, connects a focus area to an application and possible role, and chooses a small next step.

Story mapping: US01, US03, US04, US05, US06, US07, US08, US09. US02 enables self-paced presentation review.

## Shared boundaries

No student accounts, saved personal profiles, tracking, admissions advice or live AI services are needed. Both journeys use the existing static website and locally bundled demos. Independent exploration covers every focus area, irrespective of the three selected live examples.
